Frequently asked questions

Answers before the first call.

Clear terms, a simple patient experience and no need to rebuild your business around a pharmacy savings product.

Is this insurance?

No, this is a prescription cash discount program, not insurance or a substitute for insurance. Patients pay the applicable discounted cash price directly to the pharmacy.

Can patients use it alongside insurance?

Patients can compare the program's cash price with what they would pay using insurance and choose which transaction to use. A discount-card transaction cannot be combined with insurance benefits and does not apply toward an insurance deductible.

Which pharmacies accept it?

The network includes more than 60,000 participating retail pharmacies, including major national chains. Individual pharmacy participation and prescription pricing can change and should be verified at the time of use.

What does the program cost our organization?

There is a required one-time implementation fee, quoted to fit your program and setup. Physical cards, custom portal work, API integrations and custom reports are available at additional quoted cost.

How and when are we paid?

SRxS provides monthly eligible-claim reporting and a corresponding partner statement. Payment timing and conditions are set out in your partner agreement, including the Network Admin Fee Share conditions.

How long does it take to launch?

A standard digital configuration targets a launch in about 14 days once requirements, assets and agreements are complete. Custom work, integrations or additional approvals may extend the schedule.

Can price search appear on our site or app?

A branded price-search portal is part of the standard offering. Embedding or API-based access can be scoped separately to fit your website or application.

Who supports patients?

The standard program includes patient help desk support for card and program access questions. We confirm the support channels and service process during implementation.

What about state discount-program requirements?

State requirements vary and may involve disclosures, registration or other program rules. SRxS tracks applicable requirements and reviews the rules relevant to your distribution model during the discovery call.
