How a claim moves

From counter to reporting in under a second.

01

Member presents your card

Printed, digital, or in a phone wallet. No enrollment, no eligibility file, no income test.

02

Pharmacy submits at the counter

The claim routes over the national switch to your BIN/PCN — yours or ours — as a cash transaction.

03
SRxS

We adjudicate in real time

Our platform prices the claim against your contracted network rates and responds in under a second.

04

Member pays the discounted price

They pay at the counter. Nothing to file, nothing to reimburse, no deductible to meet.

05

Everything lands in reporting

Drug, pharmacy, quantity, savings, and fee — captured claim by claim, visible to you.
